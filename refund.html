Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

1. Class & Session Bookings

  • You may cancel a booking at any time from the "My Bookings" section of the app.
  • Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the session start time are eligible for a full refund.
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of the session start time are not eligible for a refund.
  • Missed sessions (no-shows) are not eligible for a refund.

2. Community Event Payments

Paid community event tickets follow the same rule: cancellations more than 24 hours before the event start are refund-eligible; cancellations within 24 hours are not.

3. Memberships & Passes

Gym and pool memberships and day passes are activated immediately after purchase and are non-refundable once activated, except where the venue is unable to honour the membership. If a venue cannot provide the purchased service, contact us and we will issue a full refund.

4. Cancelled or Changed Sessions

If a venue or organizer cancels or materially changes a session or event you paid for, you are entitled to a full refund regardless of timing.

5. How Refunds Are Processed

  • When you cancel an eligible booking, it is marked as "Refund requested" in the app.
  • Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 7–14 business days.
  • Refunds are issued in Omani Rial (OMR) for the amount paid.

6. Contact

For refund questions or issues, contact us at thenelabco@gmail.com. Include your account email and booking details so we can help quickly.
