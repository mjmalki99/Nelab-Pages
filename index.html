Nelab

Booking, payment & exploration platform for sport communities and pools in Oman.

Nelab is a mobile app that lets you discover gyms, pools, classes, and sport communities across Oman — then book and pay for them in a few taps. All bookings are confirmed instantly in the app.

Our Services

Gym & Pool Memberships

Browse gyms and swimming pools near you, compare membership plans and day passes, and purchase directly in the app. Your membership is activated immediately after payment.

Classes & Session Bookings

Book fitness classes, swimming sessions, and training sessions at partner venues. Pick a schedule, pay, and your spot is reserved instantly under "My Bookings".

Sport Community Events

Join sport communities, RSVP to community events, and pay for paid events securely in the app.

Payments

All payments are made in Omani Rial (OMR) and processed securely by Thawani, a licensed Omani payment gateway. We accept card payments (Visa / Mastercard). Nelab does not store your card details.
